# Web tool tutorial · right-column variant (second color)

A variant of the example series (the base `reading.md` explains what does not change: 19:9, text outside the image,
the 3D panel, a closing with only the logo, text pairs from `MARCA.pares`). This note holds only what the variant
changes, and why; replace it with the reading of your own product. Direction on the ten axes: `direction.json`.

| Element | Choice in the example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| background and frame | light background with two flat shapes in light shades of the second color, on the side opposite the text; a 10 px line around the screen, radius 28, short shadow | shapes stacked in tones of one family; the sentence reads on the plain background, never over a shape. The thick line replaces the browser bar |
| caption, label and badge | loose on the right, 30%, dark, `virar` change; a pill in the strong shade of the second color with light text | the text on the other side: the reading goes from the screen to the step |
| title | bold, in the strong shade of the second color, comes in through a mask | a color pair for large text only |
| opening | panel in the strong shade of the second color with the light logo; the preview beside the title; the index; `deslizar` exit (everything leaves to the left and the panel comes in from the right) | a sideways pass, without the base's flip |
| panel | `plano`: face-on, still; only the camera moves inside it | sober; the movement is the guided zoom's |
| section | card in the second color with dark text, the section number huge above the name; `empurrar`: it rises from below pushing the panel and leaves through the top | the chapter becomes a number: "02" reads from afar |
| closing | the lightest shade of the second color, only the logo, slides in from the right | the sign-off is still the logo, alone |
| highlight | outline in the strong shade of the second color | the color as a shape, never as small text |
| soundtrack | calm acoustic pop, 100–106 BPM (piano, pluck, shaker, acoustic kick), restrained foreground, −14 LUFS | a calm pace, different from the base's electronic pop |
