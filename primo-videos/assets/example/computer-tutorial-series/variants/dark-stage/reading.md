# Web tool tutorial · dark-stage variant

A variant of the example series (the base `reading.md` explains what does not change: 19:9, text outside the image,
the 3D panel, a closing with only the logo, text pairs from `MARCA.pares`). This note holds only what the variant
changes, and why; replace it with the reading of your own product. Direction on the ten axes: `direction.json`.

| Element | Choice in the example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| background and frame | dark stage with the brand symbol (its PNG, whole, in its own color), giant, cropped behind the screen and turning slowly; browser frame with a dark bar and a light address, no shadow | the symbol as a background shape, tone on tone, on the side opposite the text; the tool's light screen is the only thing lit on the stage |
| caption, label and badge | loose on the left, 28%, light on dark, `subir` change; a light pill with dark text | light text on dark; the pill lights up in the dark |
| title | bold, light, comes in word by word | the same title, on the dark stage |
| opening | panel in the dark shade of the main color with the light logo; the preview beside the title; the index; `cortina` exit in the main color | a flat-color curtain in place of the flip |
| panel | `orbita`: it turns slowly around its own center (Y and X), except during the reading zoom | an object on the stage |
| section | light card with the dark name typed out with the cursor; `mascara`: the card is born in a circle in the middle of the panel, covers the frame and closes back into it | "the light comes on" on the dark stage |
| closing | dark background, only the light logo, by dissolve | the sign-off is still the logo, alone, in the version that reads on the dark background |
| highlight | outline in the main color | the brand color as a shape |
| soundtrack | broken-beat electronic, 94–100 BPM (soft break, pad and epiano, 808 bass, sparse bell), restrained foreground, −14 LUFS | night, no dance floor |
