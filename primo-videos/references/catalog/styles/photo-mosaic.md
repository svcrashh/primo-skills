# Photo mosaic (`photo-mosaic`)

Approved photos in a grid that rearranges itself on the beat: mosaic → one photo highlighted → mosaic in another order. An
informative label changes at each beat, in a strip or in a panel of its own, **never over the photo** (the text-over-image
check stays on).

**When to use:** objectives `attention` (internal event opening, welcome), `update` (institutional, the brand's
world), `results` (what a team did, with its own photos); channels `site`, `big-screen`, `feed`; internal, institutional
and people audiences.
**When not to use:** without approved photos (only the brand's own approved photos, never stock images or people
without authorization); teaching how to use something; more than 12 photos; a photo with customer data or of a person without authorization.

## Structure (8–15 s)
- **0 s:** the photos coming in one by one, the tag and the 1st label already in the strip.
- **Beats (3–4 s each, 2–4 of them):** mosaic (grid with 1 or 2 big photos), highlight (one big photo, the others
  in a column of thumbnails), mosaic again in another order. The photos fly from the old spot to the new one one after
  the other; the label changes with them.
- **Closing:** the photos shrink, the closing color opens, only the logo, held ~2 s.

## Formats (recomposed, not cropped)
- **16:9:** mosaic with the label strip below; or split, label panel on the left and photos on the right.
- **9:16:** transposed grid; strip at the bottom (inside the Reels area) or panel on top.
- **1:1:** like 9:16.

## The request must include
3 to 12 approved photos (and the focus of each one, if the face isn't in the center); 2 to 4 short, true labels
(name, number, fact); the dominant color.

## Variants
- **mosaic + strip, still camera:** a grid with one big photo, light strip below (`example.json`).
- **split + tracking:** a color panel with the big label; the grid slides slowly and bleeds off the edge
  (`example-split.json`).
- **guided zoom:** the highlighted photo slowly pushes in on its focus.
