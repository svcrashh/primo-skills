# Chapters with index (`chapters-with-index`)

Opening with the logo and a numbered index, one chapter per new feature with the redesigned interface big and animated, closing
with the complete index and big numbers. For a list the person wants to **go through**.

**When to use:** objectives `update` and `results` (long year recap); channel `site` (website, YouTube, help
center); product `creator`, `b2b`, `game` (a chapter becomes an update item). From 6 to 12 new features, each with a
visible result in the interface.
**Rule:** **never recommended twice in a row for the same product.** If the product's last video was this one,
recommend `rolling-list` (see `../choosing.md`).
**When not to use:** a single new feature; a where-to-click tutorial (the interface is redesigned, not the real one); short feed and
Reels videos (cut one chapter per video); a `young` audience that won't read the supporting text.
**Voice:** optional (opt-in, paid; `references/voice-script.md`). Each chapter lasts as long as its line, and each stage
action and support line comes in on the word that names it. How: `references/chapters.md` §9 to §11.

## Structure (1 min 30 s to 3 min)
- **Opening (5–8 s):** the logo, the video's word ("What's new") and the numbered index filling in item by item.
  The index shows how many new features there are (with voice, the opening line says it too).
- **Chapters (5–12 s each):** transition from the index card (~0.9 s) → tag `NN · AREA` + poster-card title of
  2–3 words + 1 to 3 supporting lines → the stage with the redesigned interface doing an action every 1.5–3 s →
  final state held ≥ 1.2 s. With voice, each action lands on the word that names it.
- **More than 12:** the rest becomes "And more", one chapter with three small new features.
- **Closing:** the complete index can come back as the last content frame; the closing itself is, by default, **only the brand's logo**,
  centered and held ~2 s (the brand's slogan beside it only when the brand has one and the person wants it).

## Formats (recomposed, not cropped)
- **16:9:** text on the left, stage on the right.
- **9:16:** title on top, stage below.
- **1:1:** title and supporting text on top, wide stage below.
No stage text smaller than 22 px in the final frame.

## The request must include
The list of new features (6–12) with the exact label of each button or field; an example user with a photo and real
numbers; logo; date; the 2–3 numbers for the closing; every claim checked in production. With voice, also the risk
words (product and proper names, acronyms, terms in another language) and how each one is said.

## Variants
Tone of the poster-card title (sober for B2B, shouted for creators and games); how much people (photo) and data the
stage shows; the soundtrack's tempo. Composition changing every 3–4 chapters is left for a version 2.

## How to render
`$SKILL/references/chapters.md` (read it in full) and `$SKILL/assets/chapters/README.md` (the example template).
The template comes with no brand (`assets/chapters/template/`, `assets/chapters/scenes/`): the brand's tokens, logo
component and fonts are copied from the product into the project's kit before the first chapter.
With voice, also `$SKILL/references/voice-script.md` (the lines), `voice-choice.md` (the voice, the key and the cost)
and `voice-mix.md` (the soundtrack that gives way to the voice).
