# Light-trail teaser (`light-trail-teaser`)

Dark background; a line of light draws pieces of the interface and turns into the next element; magnified fragments, never the
whole screen; the reveal comes at the end. It's the template made to be remembered.

**When to use:** objectives `attention` and `launch` (big version, new identity, season), `update` on a big screen;
channels `big-screen`, `site`, `feed`; product `game`, `b2b` with an audience that appreciates polish (designers, devs), creators.
Before launching, with a set date.
**When not to use:** tutorial; a product that's already live (it frustrates whoever wants to see it); a light interface full of text
with nothing pretty up close; a conservative or lay audience (banking, school, price-driven retail: it looks like a party); whoever needs to
understand what it is before getting interested.
**Voice:** optional (opt-in, paid; `references/voice-script.md`). A line on the promise and one on the closing, each
from its `em`, with the music giving way 10 dB under them. How: `templates/light-trail-teaser/TEMPLATE.md`, "With voice
(optional)".

## Structure (15–45 s)
- **0–3 s:** dark, with the promise or the date already written small; the line rises and draws the edge of the first panel,
  which appears behind it.
- **Fragments (2–5 s each, 4–6 of them):** pieces of the UI in close-up (2–3×), cut off at the edge, over a dark background with
  blurred shapes moving slowly. The line leaves one fragment and turns into the next (a button, a field).
- **Cards (~1 s each):** "NEW · FEATURE NAME" in small mono type, lots of air around it.
- **Reveal (3–4 s):** the new thing whole in the center, or a wall of screens pulling away.
- **Closing:** a 2–6 word sentence lighting up word by word; "coming <date>" + brand.

## Formats (recomposed, not cropped)
- **16:9:** the line sweeps horizontally; fragment at ~60% of the width, cut off at the edge.
- **9:16:** the line goes vertical, fragment at ~85% of the width in the center; cards on 2 lines.
- **1:1:** fragment at 2–3× in the center, cards the same as 16:9.

## The request must include
4–6 new features with a short name; the real screen (or the component) of each one to magnify, in a dark theme or with
permission to darken it; the date or the "when"; the closing sentence; logo.

## Variants
- **line of light** (the default, looks like an event).
- **fragments + NEW cards** (faster, ~1 s per fragment for games and a young audience).
- **reveal in parts:** blurred outline → one detail → the new thing whole on the central axis.
- **the brand draws itself:** the line draws the new logo (new identity, renaming).
- B2B: a single color and more time on each fragment; games: saturated color and faster cuts.

## How to render
`$SKILL/templates/light-trail-teaser/TEMPLATE.md`. Format examples: `example.json` (three fragments with a number, a
list and a real screen, the phone reveal, closing with the logo alone), `example-steps.json` (four fast steps and the
brand symbol giant in the corner) and `example-photo.json` (a photo fragment and the closing that opens in a circle).
