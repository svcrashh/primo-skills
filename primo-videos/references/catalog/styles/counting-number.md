# Counting number (`counting-number`)

One big number, on its own, that climbs to the final value, and then a three-word label that says what it counts.
It works as a video of its own of 6–20 s or as an opening or closing block for any other template.

**When to use:** objectives `results` (the main one), `update` (a number or a rule changed), `launch` (offer,
price, deadline), `attention`; channels `whatsapp`, `feed`, `big-screen`, `reels`; product `b2b` (sober), `retail` (marquee),
`game` (balancing), `finance`, `creator`.
**When not to use:** a new product with no number of its own; a small or invented number (it sounds like an excuse); a sensitive
subject (health, the customer's money) where counting sounds like bragging. **The number has to be true.**

## Structure (6–20 s)
- **0–2 s:** the giant number on its own, counting up to the final value ("+" or "%" already in place). No label yet: that's
  what hooks.
- **2–5 s:** the 3–5 word label ("improvements this year"); if there is one, the list of what it counts scrolling below.
- **5–15 s:** 1–3 more numbers on color cards, ~1.5 s each, or the number coming back as a chorus.
- **Closing:** number + product + logo, held ≥ 1.5 s.
As a block: only the 0–5 s, in the opening or closing of another template.

## Formats (recomposed, not cropped)
- **16:9:** number at ~50–70% of the height in the center; label below or beside it.
- **9:16:** number at ~35% of the height, label and list below.
- **1:1:** number at ~55% of the height, label on one line.

## The request must include
The true number (and the "before", if you'll show before → after); what it counts in 3–5 words; the list of
items, if there is one; for an offer, the condition and the legal text; for cards, 2–4 numbers and the palette.

## Variants
- **counter + scrolling list** (year-end wrap-up).
- **three numbers on cards** of color, ~1.5 s each.
- **parameter before → after:** tag `NAME  30 → 40` over the screen in use, old value dimmed, new one strong.
- **number as a chorus:** the same figure comes back every ~4 s in another typeface (15–25 s ad).
- **offer on a marquee:** giant price or deadline (35–40% of the height) coming in like a stamp over the app.
- **number running on the screen:** balance, invoice, steps counting inside the redesigned app.
- **sheet that slides out of the slot:** the number revealed with 3 s of suspense.

## How to render
`$SKILL/templates/counting-number/TEMPLATE.md`. Format examples: `example.json` (three formats, counter with a scrolling
list and color cards), `example-whip.json` (whip transition, the giant symbol in the background) and
`example-push.json` (one number per screen, push transition, the camera moving in).
