# Typing text (`typing-text`)

The question or the request appears written on screen from frame 0, with a cursor, letter by letter; and the answer is the
product at work. It meets rule 1 on its own and it's the engine's cheapest style.

**When to use:** objectives `launch`, `teach` (explaining what the product is), `attention`; channels `reels`, `whatsapp`,
`feed`, `site`; product `young`, and any product with a **text box** (AI, search, chat,
sign-up). Works without sound.
**When not to use:** a product whose appeal is visual and doesn't fit in a sentence (action games, camera, fashion); listing many
new features; a where-to-click tutorial (the text takes the place of the screen).

## Structure (15–60 s)
- **0–2 s:** plain background, a big field or cursor, and the first word already being typed (never a cursor blinking
  alone for seconds).
- **2–4 s cycles:** types (10–15 letters/s, autocomplete all at once) → result → the camera magnifies the line that
  matters. 4–6 cycles in 30 s.
- **Turn:** the last request is the one that changes the meaning (the kind answer, the "yes", the number).
- **Closing (3–4 s):** a 2–4 word sentence on a plain background + logo.

## Formats (recomposed, not cropped)
- **16:9:** field at 45–90% of the width in the center; previous and next examples blurred above and below (carousel).
- **9:16:** field at 85% of the width in the top third; answer stacked below; vertical carousel.
- **1:1:** field and answer on the same screen, field on top.

## The request must include
3 to 12 real requests that tell the story (that's the script); the answer to each one (recorded or
redesigned screen); the final sentence. For A/B: a comparable, true number. For "what it isn't": 2–3 things the
product isn't.

## Variants
- **box with carousel:** launch; examples scroll by, one gets typed, the result assembles itself, a final grid of results.
- **text that leads:** the typed sentence explains the product; "it's not X" pills crossed out, then the screen proof.
- **A/B with a number:** two boxes side by side, a badge with the number on each ("without / with").
- **the screen answers:** the audience's question as a big caption, the app's answer written on screen, ping-pong.
- **story through the interface:** someone's life told only through what they type into the product, without narration.

## How to render
`$SKILL/templates/typing-text/TEMPLATE.md`. Format examples: `example.json` (one typed request and the product's
answer as a card), `example-side.json` (two requests with the answer beside them, a card and then numbers) and
`example-phrases.json` (typed sentences only, with do and don't pills).
