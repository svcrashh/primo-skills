# Before and after (`before-after`)

The same screen, in the same framing, in two states, cut on the click. The eye compares on its own; there's no need
to explain. Variant: zoom-out, where the detail changes meaning when the whole thing appears.

**When to use:** objectives `attention`, `results`, `teach` (a single step), `update` (redesign); channels `whatsapp`,
`reels`, `site`; product `b2b` (with the "after" held still to read), `game`, `retail`, `young`. A feature that
transforms something visible in one step: organize, filter, clean up, turn into a chart, fill in.
**When not to use:** invisible feature (security, performance, integration: use `counting-number`); when the
"before" is already good and the difference is subtle; a process with many steps (it becomes a tutorial); comparison with a named competitor.
**Voice:** optional (opt-in, paid; `references/voice-script.md`). The "before" and "after" lines, read by the voice, set
the time up to the click and the caption follows the words; the MP4 then carries the voice only, without the
soundtrack. How: `templates/before-after/TEMPLATE.md`, "With voice (optional)".

## Structure (8–30 s)
- **0–2 s:** the written promise and the "before" already on screen (ugly and true).
- **2–6 s:** the camera moves into the UI up to the area that will change; big cursor up to the button.
- **The turn (~1 s):** hard cut on the click, same place, same size; one word on screen ("Done.", "Easy.").
- **After (2–3 s):** the result held still to read (B2B holds longer; social media, shorter).
- **Closing:** sentence + logo. Optional: the "with × without" side by side, held ~5 s.

## Formats (recomposed, not cropped)
- **16:9:** the feature panel on the left (~25%) and the work area on the right (~75%), caption in a pill at the bottom;
  or "with × without" in two halves with a 2-word tag over each.
- **9:16:** the screen on top (~50%) and the zoomed-in detail or the step card below; caption in the middle.
- **1:1:** a curtain dragged between the two states, or two halves side by side.

## The request must include
A true "before" (file or screen state), the feature that transforms, the "after" recorded in the **same
viewport**, and a turn word. For the zoom-out: a high-resolution capture (2× or 3×) and a turn that
is true.

## Variants
- **cut on the click** (the default).
- **with × without side by side**, held long enough to compare.
- **zoom-out that reveals:** starts on a detail magnified 20–40× and pulls back until the context changes the reading.
- **pull-back that reveals:** from the detail to the whole to show scale ("10 thousand rows without freezing").
- **before and now together:** the old version and the new one doing the same thing (redesign, anniversary).
- **the same place over time:** captures by day ("DAY 01 … DAY 34").
- **proof with zoom:** the camera goes to the detail that proves it (instead of the arrow, scrim + blur).
