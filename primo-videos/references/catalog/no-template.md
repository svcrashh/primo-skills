# Still without a template

Styles and ideas the study found that don't have a template yet. **You can suggest any of them as an idea**, but tell
the user, in the same sentence, that the video comes out in the closest template (named on each line) and what changes.
Example: "This could be told as a 'reaction case film'; since I don't have that template yet, it comes out in
`counting-number` with the response wall from `rolling-list`". Never promise anything listed under "out of reach"
(`rules.md`).

## Already a variant of a template

If the request uses one of these names, it's the template shown, in the variant with that name:
- **`real-screen-camera`:** device in the scene, from laptop to detail, camera that roams the UI, floating window
  with macro, real screen with step card, lightning tutorial with karaoke caption.
- **`typing-text`:** field that types itself, prompt that turns into the product, typed text leads, typed text
  in color, story told by the interface, the screen answers.
- **`rolling-list`:** feature burst, lightning list, rolling numbered list, phrase card and lightning, features in
  the feed, dismantled UI and wall, phrase that lights up, sliding card stack, name that decodes itself, action
  close-ups in sequence, promise at the top, "new" tag in the corner, isolated component in the dark.
- **`counting-number`:** number as a chorus, offer on a marquee, before → after tag, running number, total that
  climbs, offer strip and card, scoreboard and fact tag, sheet that slides out of the slot.
- **`before-after`:** before and after on screen, object that changes over time, zoom-out that reveals, pull-back
  that reveals, before and now together, matched split screen, with and without side by side, proof with zoom and
  arrow (without the arrow: scrim and blur).
- **`light-trail-teaser`:** line of light, fragment teaser, reveal in parts.

## Styles without a template

**Tutorial and demo**
- Concept before the screen — explains the idea (interest, subscription) before showing the button — **template: `drawn-explainer`**; for the real button after the idea, follow with `real-screen-camera`.
- Window with a side column — long demo (3–5 min) with something always visible beside the screen — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`**.
- Split screen with text that writes itself — half device, half text that sells — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`**.
- Tour in a mockup frame over a gradient — new SaaS version shown in 4–6 areas, screen in a mockup frame — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`**.
- UI floating on color — one feature or a short flow for people who already know the brand, UI over flat color — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`** (floating window).
- Camera over the tilted UI — new feature in perspective, for a demanding audience — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`** (without the tilt).
- Vertical demo in three layers — feature demo for Reels: face, phone, full screen — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`** (9:16, without the face).
- Caption anchored to the device — "now also on…", the product on several screens — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`**.
- Vertical feed inside 16:9 — the app's vertical screen or users' posts in a horizontal video — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`**.
- Vertical screen with credit — reveals a user's real use after a dramatization — **falls into the closest template: `real-screen-camera`**.

**What's new and launch**
- Name card and burst — launches 2–5 things at once, like a trailer — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`**.
- Showcase of what it makes — product with a visual result (website, photo, invitation) shown through what comes out of it — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`** (burst of results).
- 6-second bumper — shows that a collection exists (themes, skins, colors) — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`** (burst).
- Numbered tips — 60–70 s of tips people save and share — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`** (list, 2–3 s per item, or more).
- Word triptych — three windows, three words, one sentence that completes itself — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`**.
- Metaphor with the data — an abstract new feature becomes a drawing made with the product's real names — **template: `drawn-explainer`** (the drawing appears on the word in the line, with the product's real names).
- The brand draws itself — new identity or renaming, the brand traced in front of the viewer — **falls into the closest template: `light-trail-teaser`**.
- Phone full of typography — "coming soon" with a date, phone covered in text — **falls into the closest template: `light-trail-teaser`**.
- Single object on an empty screen — one strange thing on its own asks for an action (scanning a QR) — **falls into the closest template: `light-trail-teaser`**.
- Diagonal split screen — a choice between two modes or plans (free × pro) — **falls into the closest template: `before-after`** (side by side).
- Color breather with a fixed phrase — brand presence post with a famous color — **falls into the closest template: `typing-text`**.

**Social proof and accountability** (second batch, see below)
- Testimonial in a mockup frame — a real customer case in 30–40 s, the screen comes in when they mention it — **falls into the closest template: `typing-text`** (the customer's sentence written out, with the screen).
- Card and wall — "we asked 100 people", ends on the volume of answers — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`** (wall).
- Mosaic that multiplies — scale without a number ("thousands of customers") — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`** (wall).
- Mosaic of places — "where we are", city by city — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`** (wall).
- Album on white — short story with photos of real people — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`** (poster card and proof with photos).
- Reactions / repercussion case film — an action that already happened, told through reactions and numbers — **falls into the closest template: `counting-number`**.
- Recap with a player bar — something long (event, year, season) summed up in 60–90 s — **falls into the closest template: `chapters-with-index`**.

**Short humor for Reels** (second batch, see below)
- Notification that tells the story — an everyday situation told through the app's notification, 8–15 s — **falls into the closest template: `typing-text`**.
- Security camera (caught in the act) — the humor twist, "the moment of the mistake" — **falls into the closest template: `before-after`**.
- TV news ticker — a new feature announced as news, with humor — **falls into the closest template: `rolling-list`**.
- Reward that travels — the purchase turns into a coin that flies to the balance — **falls into the closest template: `counting-number`**.

**Openings, closings and captions** (pieces, not styles: they fit in any template)
- Logo that turns into the app, logo in steps — 2–3 s opening: "this is an app" before the first word — **falls into the closest template: any** (opening).
- Giant end card, download card over the scene — closing with the requested action — **falls into the closest template: any** (closing).
- Giant keyword, chunk caption with a bold number, narrating caption, caption made of the product — the caption kit from rule 4 — **falls into the closest template: any** (caption).

## Ideas

The idea is the story; the style is how it shows up. Choose the idea when the request asks for a hook, a twist or a
joke, and tell it with the style shown. First come the ones that translate best into product video, without filming
anyone.

**The most useful ones**
- Journey through what gets typed — someone's life (or problem) told only through what they type into the product — `typing-text`.
- Truth that flips — good facts until the audience agrees; the revealed context changes the meaning — `before-after` (zoom-out).
- The product's little magic — three attempts the old way fail; the fourth, in the app, looks like magic — `before-after`.
- The problem has a face — the invisible pain becomes an exaggerated villain, and one tap in the app makes it disappear — `before-after`.
- Eight-second situation — a scene everyone has lived, with a real message from the app as the line — `typing-text`.
- Reply to a comment — someone's real question in a comment bubble, and the brand answers on the spot — `typing-text`.
- Flaw becomes proof — show the ugly thing on purpose because it proves the promise — `before-after`.
- Day count — the same place, day after day ("DAY 01 … DAY 34"); time is the proof — `before-after`.
- They say no, the app says yes — "they say I'll never…", and the final answer comes from the app itself — `typing-text`.
- What I say × what I think — the polite sentence and, right after, what you actually think; the product solves the second one — `typing-text`.

**Hook and opening**
- Define by what it isn't — three things the product isn't, and back to the opening sentence — `typing-text`.
- Ad that's already over — brand and promise in the first 5 s; the rest is a gift for whoever stayed — `typing-text`.
- Door that opens — one hook, one reveal, the end, in under 12 s — `light-trail-teaser`.
- Unfinished sentence that begs for the end — frame 0 starts "X is…" and only completes at the end — `typing-text` (or `light-trail-teaser`).
- The customer's question as the title — the real doubt in quotes at the start; the answer comes in numbers that grow — `counting-number`.
- What it feels like to X — "when you…", a situation everyone has lived; the brand stamped on top, and the useful tip — `typing-text`.
- The insult becomes the setting — the worst nickname the internet gave the brand, in big letters in frame 0, becomes a story — `typing-text`.
- Epic, then the real screen — a small moment of use dramatized like a film, and at the end the real screen that inspired it — `light-trail-teaser`.

**Objection and answer**
- Objection ahead of time — right after the promise, answers the distrust ("and it's not a promotion") and shows the way — `real-screen-camera`.
- Each objection becomes a character — each doubt of someone about to download asks, the product answers, final list with the button — `typing-text`.
- The skeptics vs. the product — each objection said out loud and answered with a fact on screen — `rolling-list` (or `chapters-with-index`, one chapter per objection).
- "They say…" and the image proves them wrong — the criticism of the category answered with images only — `before-after`.
- Debunk with proof — the rumor, the posts that spread it, and "the answer is no" — `typing-text`.
- The fear that becomes a feature — every threat the user imagines is answered by something useful and mundane in the product — `typing-text` (the screen answers).
- Advice before the sale — 80% of the video teaches how not to fall into a trap; the product comes in at the end as the honest way — `typing-text`.
- Answering a complaint — the real complaint in 1.5 s and a short answer that turns it in your favor (with mockery only if the brand already has that tone) — `typing-text`.
- The counter question — a short, real question repeated until it becomes the brand's line — `typing-text`.

**List, chorus and series**
- Trailer that is a list — the version name bursts onto the screen, and the new features come with a tag in the same corner — `rolling-list`.
- What's coming this month — invitation card, N items of ~3 s, logo; 30 s without voice — `rolling-list`.
- List that turns into the product — a wish list where the last item looks like something else, until it reveals the product — `rolling-list`.
- Catchy jingle with a list — lists things with a chorus that comes back and the message in the last item (spoken chorus) — `rolling-list`.
- Jingle of moments — everyday situations, all closing on the same chorus, which is the product's name — `rolling-list`.
- Chorus that repeats the promise — "when X gets hard → the product makes it easy", repeated between situations — `rolling-list`.
- One promise per scene — each scene with a written promise (deadline, stock, coupon), name and date repeated — `rolling-list`.
- This is also — a thesis sentence repeated in every place where the product lives, broken by a joke — `rolling-list`.
- Genre metalanguage — imitates the clichés of its own kind of video and shows the product was in all of them — `rolling-list`.
- The interface the audience already knows — tells the product with the grammar of a famous app (swiping, stories) — `rolling-list` (card stack).
- The list of lives — a first-person voice tells everything it has been; the past proves the future — `rolling-list`.
- The brand's whole city — a fixed universe that comes back every "season" (recurring update) — `chapters-with-index`.
- Series that calls the next one — every short video ends with the same question-invitation — any template (it's the closing).
- Anniversary becomes a place — a milestone becomes a "party", and each short video visits a corner of it — `counting-number` (the milestone), as a series.

**Number and proof**
- Weird user stat — a real, strange usage number, and a dry sentence that comments on it — `counting-number`.
- The number built from pieces — the offer number built from two familiar things (10 + 9 = 19) — `counting-number`.
- The sum that ends without a price — items with prices add up, and the last one can't be measured — `counting-number`.
- Capped challenge — a challenge with a limit ("dinner for under R$20") solved in the app, with an invitation to the audience — `counting-number`.
- Pride of the obsessive — the product owns up to being for people who take the subject too seriously: "it's not for everyone" — `counting-number`.
- Question and answer with a number — the feature answers a real question with a personal stat ("13 times in 180 days") — `typing-text`.
- Live proof, absurd — a test so extreme it becomes a spectacle, result side by side — `before-after`.
- The whole world using it — place after place until a final mosaic with each one's name — `rolling-list` (wall).
- Public fight between brands — short jabs, each with a number that hurts (only with an agreed partnership) — `counting-number`.
- Taunt the rival, through the app / Use the rival as a stage — the promotion only works on the competitor's turf; the news becomes the ad — `counting-number`.
- The proof is the video itself — the whole video was made with the product, and 1 s behind-the-scenes flashes prove it — `rolling-list`.

**Transformation**
- One task from start to finish — the app does a complete task, in the real order, and only at the end says what it is — `real-screen-camera`.
- Talks to the viewer — the voice gives orders ("now tap here"), and the viewer becomes a character — `real-screen-camera`.
- Little find that fixes an annoyance — "expensive result with a cheap thing" in frame 0, steps with tags, proof side by side — `real-screen-camera` (closing in `before-after`).
- Try, try, it works — tries a "power" several times; on the last one, the product makes it work — `before-after`.
- The old way taken to the absurd — the old way exaggerated until someone says there's an easy way — `before-after`.
- Old and new side by side — the old version and the new one doing the same thing — `before-after`.
- Generations in one film — the product's history, decade by decade, up to today — `before-after`.
- You're not you / The problem turns into another person — with the problem, the person becomes someone else; the product brings them back — `before-after`.
- Two characters compare — "our way" and "the old way" as two fixed characters, one argument per episode — `before-after`.
- Result first, closes in a loop — opens on the "after", shows each action in under 1 s and goes back to the opening frame — `before-after`.
- The zoom that reveals the impossible — so tight you can't tell what it is; the pull-back reveals the absurd thing that proves the product — `before-after` (zoom-out).
- The small thing that crosses the big world — something tiny carries the order through huge settings to someone — `before-after` (pull-back).
- New screen without a scare — the redesign shown first only in the part that changed, isolated, then the full path — `rolling-list` (isolated component), then `real-screen-camera`.
- The forgetful one who needs the product — someone forgets and gets everything wrong; the product is "what remembers for you" — `typing-text` (the notification).

**Brand and emotion**
- Enemy without showing the product — the gray world the product will break; the product only in the final text, with a date — `light-trail-teaser`.
- Who will be number one — retrospective teaser with a contest ("who will be your #1?"), without revealing — `light-trail-teaser`.
- Intruder in someone else's universe — the brand walks into the world of a date or launch everyone is watching — `light-trail-teaser`.
- Manifesto in "we" — "when X, we Y" sentences from the community; the last word crossed out and replaced by the product's — `typing-text`.
- Manifesto about the people who use it — a short text that praises the people who use it; the product only in the sign-off — `rolling-list` (phrase that lights up).
- The truth nobody believes — someone says sincerely why they use it, and nobody believes them — `typing-text`.
- The world talks about you — what others post and report about the product's world, with humor — `rolling-list`.

**Need people, a mascot or real material** (only with the material in hand)
- True story in 30 s, Photo album of people who used it, The proof is the audience — need a testimonial, photos or real answers; without that, don't invent: use `counting-number`.
- The brand becomes a verse, The logo becomes a gesture, Deliberate blooper, Character with attitude — need filmed people: out of reach today.
- Threatening mascot — needs a layered SVG mascot (second batch); until then, the threat becomes a notification in `typing-text`.

## Second batch

What comes after the 8 templates:
- **Social proof and accountability:** testimonial in a mockup frame, response wall, case film, mosaic of places,
  recap with a player bar. Depends on real material from the customer (answers, screenshots, recorded testimonial),
  **never invented**. Until then: `counting-number` + `rolling-list` (wall) + `before-after`.
- **8-second situation:** notification that tells the story, security camera, TV news ticker, reward that travels:
  the short-humor kit for Reels. Until then: `typing-text` or `before-after`.
- **Chapters, version 2:** the composition changing every 3–4 chapters and the index off the right side.
- **2D SVG mascot:** only when the request brings the mascot in layers: a 5 s sting, gesture and expression only.
  With voice, a second step after the sting: speech with 4–5 mouth shapes timed from the voice's word alignment.
  A mascot that's only a PNG doesn't get started (splitting it into parts is an illustrator's job).
